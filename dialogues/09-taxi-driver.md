# Taxi Driver (1976)

> **Travis Bickle:** "You talkin' to me? You talkin' to me? You talkin' to me? Then who the hell else are you talkin'—you talkin' to me? Well, I'm the only one here. Who the fuck do you think you're talking to?"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Character** | Travis Bickle |
| **Actor** | Robert De Niro |
| **Director** | Martin Scorsese |

**Context:** Alone in his apartment, Travis rehearses a confrontation with his own reflection in the mirror.

**Why it endures:** An unsettling portrait of isolation that is now among the most imitated moments in film.
