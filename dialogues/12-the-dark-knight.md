# The Dark Knight (2008)

> "You either die a hero, or you live long enough to see yourself become the villain."

| | |
|---|---|
| **Character** | Harvey Dent |
| **Actor** | Aaron Eckhart |
| **Director** | Christopher Nolan |

**Context:** At a dinner table, the district attorney reflects on how the public treats its heroes.

**Why it endures:** It foreshadows Dent's own fall into Two-Face, and its warning about heroism has been quoted far beyond the film.
