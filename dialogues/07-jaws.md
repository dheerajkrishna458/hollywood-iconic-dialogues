# Jaws (1975)

> **Chief Martin Brody:** "You're gonna need a bigger boat."
>
> **Matt Hooper:** "Did you see that? Look at the size of him! Twenty-footer!"
>
> **Quint:** "Twenty-five. Three tons of him."

| | |
|---|---|
| **Character** | Chief Martin Brody |
| **Actor** | Roy Scheider |
| **Director** | Steven Spielberg |

**Context:** While chumming the water from the Orca, Brody comes face to face with the shark and quietly tells Quint.

**Why it endures:** Dry, deadpan dread. The line is widely reported to have been improvised, and it is the film's best-known joke.
