# The Lion King (1994)

> **Timon:** "Hakuna Matata! What a wonderful phrase!"
>
> **Pumbaa:** "Hakuna Matata! Ain't no passing craze!"
>
> **Timon:** "It means no worries for the rest of your days."
>
> **Timon & Pumbaa:** "It's our problem-free philosophy—Hakuna Matata!"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Character** | Timon & Pumbaa |
| **Actor** | Nathan Lane & Ernie Sabella |
| **Director** | Roger Allers & Rob Minkoff |

**Context:** After rescuing a young, guilt-ridden Simba in the desert, meerkat Timon and warthog Pumbaa teach him their laid-back Swahili motto for leaving the past behind.

**Why it endures:** Whether watched in English or dubbed in regional languages across India, "Hakuna Matata" is one of the most universally known feel-good phrases in animation history.
