# The Matrix (1999)

> **Morpheus:** "This is your last chance. After this, there is no turning back. You take the blue pill—the story ends, you wake up in your bed and believe whatever you want to believe. You take the red pill—you stay in Wonderland, and I show you how deep the rabbit hole goes. Remember: all I'm offering is the truth. Nothing more."

| | |
|---|---|
| **Character** | Morpheus |
| **Actor** | Laurence Fishburne |
| **Director** | The Wachowskis |

**Context:** Sitting across from computer hacker Thomas Anderson (Neo), Morpheus holds out two capsules in his palms and asks Neo to choose between comfortable illusion and harsh reality.

**Why it endures:** "Red pill or blue pill" entered global everyday vocabulary—especially across tech and engineering teams in the US and India—as shorthand for a life-altering awakening.
