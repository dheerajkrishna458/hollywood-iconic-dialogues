# The Godfather (1972)

> "I'm gonna make him an offer he can't refuse."

| | |
|---|---|
| **Character** | Vito Corleone |
| **Actor** | Marlon Brando |
| **Director** | Francis Ford Coppola |

**Context:** Don Vito reassures his godson, the singer Johnny Fontane, that his career problem will be taken care of.

**Why it endures:** Menace delivered as calm courtesy. The "offer" is anything but optional, and the line became shorthand for coercion with a smile.
