# Scarface (1983)

> "Say hello to my little friend!"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Character** | Tony Montana |
| **Actor** | Al Pacino |
| **Director** | Brian De Palma |

**Context:** In his final stand, Tony defends his mansion from assassins with a rifle-mounted grenade launcher.

**Why it endures:** An over-the-top finale line that has been quoted and parodied endlessly in pop culture.
