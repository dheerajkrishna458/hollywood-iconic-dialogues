# A Few Good Men (1992)

> "You can't handle the truth!"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Character** | Col. Nathan R. Jessup |
| **Actor** | Jack Nicholson |
| **Director** | Rob Reiner |

**Context:** In the courtroom showdown, Jessup is cornered by Lt. Daniel Kaffee (Tom Cruise) and finally snaps.

**Why it endures:** The villain effectively confesses while justifying himself, and Nicholson's explosive delivery made it a staple of impressions.
