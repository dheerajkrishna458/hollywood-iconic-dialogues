# Inception (2010)

> **Cobb:** "What is the most resilient parasite? Bacteria? A virus? An intestinal worm?"
>
> **Arthur:** "What Mr. Cobb is trying to say—"
>
> **Cobb:** "An idea. Resilient… highly contagious. Once an idea has taken hold of the brain it's almost impossible to eradicate. An idea that is fully formed—fully understood—that sticks; right in there somewhere."

| | |
|---|---|
| **Character** | Dom Cobb & Arthur |
| **Actor** | Leonardo DiCaprio & Joseph Gordon-Levitt |
| **Director** | Christopher Nolan |

**Context:** Inside a shared dream, extractor Dom Cobb pitches corporate magnate Saito on the power—and danger—of planting or stealing ideas from the subconscious.

**Why it endures:** Christopher Nolan enjoys a massive fanbase in both the US and India, and this speech sets up one of modern cinema's most debated mind-benders.
