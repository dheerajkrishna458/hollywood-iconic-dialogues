# Life of Pi (2012)

> **Adult Pi Patel:** "I suppose in the end, the whole of life becomes an act of letting go, but what always hurts the most is not taking a moment to say goodbye."
>
> **Writer:** "So your story does have a happy ending."
>
> **Adult Pi Patel:** "Well, that's up to you."

| | |
|---|---|
| **Character** | Adult Piscine Molitor "Pi" Patel & The Writer |
| **Actor** | Irrfan Khan & Rafe Spall |
| **Director** | Ang Lee |

**Context:** Recounting how the Bengal tiger Richard Parker walked into the Mexican jungle without looking back after 227 days adrift together on a lifeboat, adult Pi reflects on survival, faith, and loss.

**Why it endures:** Delivered with unforgettable warmth by the late Irrfan Khan—an actor deeply revered in both Indian cinema and Hollywood (*Jurassic World*, *The Namesake*, *Slumdog Millionaire*)—this reflection resonates universally.
