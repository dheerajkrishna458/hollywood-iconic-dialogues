# Casablanca (1942)

> "Here's looking at you, kid."

| | |
|---|---|
| **Character** | Rick Blaine |
| **Actor** | Humphrey Bogart |
| **Director** | Michael Curtiz |

**Context:** Rick's toast to Ilsa Lund, first in the Paris flashback and again at the film's heartbreaking farewell.

**Why it endures:** A casual-sounding phrase that carries a whole love story, and a classic example of understated romance in Hollywood's golden age.
