# Avengers: Endgame (2019)

> **Thanos:** "I am… inevitable."
>
> *(Thanos snaps his fingers, but nothing happens. He turns the gauntlet over to see the Infinity Stones are gone—now glowing on Tony Stark's armor.)*
>
> **Tony Stark:** "And I… am… Iron Man."

| | |
|---|---|
| **Character** | Tony Stark / Iron Man & Thanos |
| **Actor** | Robert Downey Jr. & Josh Brolin |
| **Director** | Anthony and Joe Russo |

**Context:** At the climax of the Battle of Earth, Tony Stark steals the Infinity Stones from Thanos and sacrifices his own life with a snap to save the universe.

**Why it endures:** It brings the 11-year, 22-film Infinity Saga full circle by echoing Tony's final line from the original *Iron Man* (2008)—a moment that had audiences cheering in packed theaters from New York to Mumbai.
