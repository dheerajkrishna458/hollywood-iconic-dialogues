# Gone with the Wind (1939)

> **Scarlett O'Hara:** "Rhett, if you go, where shall I go? What shall I do?"
>
> **Rhett Butler:** "Frankly, my dear, I don't give a damn."

| | |
|---|---|
| **Character** | Rhett Butler |
| **Actor** | Clark Gable |
| **Director** | Victor Fleming |

**Context:** Rhett's parting reply when Scarlett O'Hara asks what will become of her if he walks out.

**Why it endures:** One of cinema's most famous exits. The mild swear word was daring for its era, which added to its impact.
