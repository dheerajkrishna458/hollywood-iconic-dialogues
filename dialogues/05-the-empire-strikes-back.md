# Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back (1980)

> "No, I am your father."

| | |
|---|---|
| **Character** | Darth Vader |
| **Actor** | David Prowse (body), James Earl Jones (voice) |
| **Director** | Irvin Kershner |

**Context:** During the Cloud City duel, Vader reveals the truth about Luke Skywalker's parentage.

**Why it endures:** The biggest twist in blockbuster history. It is also routinely misquoted as "Luke, I am your father."
