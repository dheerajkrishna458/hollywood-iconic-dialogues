# Gladiator (2000)

> "My name is Maximus Decimus Meridius… and I will have my vengeance, in this life or the next."

| | |
|---|---|
| **Character** | Maximus Decimus Meridius |
| **Actor** | Russell Crowe |
| **Director** | Ridley Scott |

**Context:** Unmasked in the arena, the enslaved former general finally tells the usurper emperor Commodus exactly who he is.

**Why it endures:** It turns a list of stripped-away titles into a vow of revenge, and it is one of the most quoted speeches of the 2000s.
