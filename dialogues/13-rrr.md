# RRR (2022)

> **Jake:** "Right, don't embarrass yourself, lad. Salsa, flamenco—takes years to master. Wait till I finish, then you can do your… whatever you call it."
>
> **Bheem:** "Naatu?"
>
> **Raju:** "Not salsa, not flamenco, my brother. Do you know Naatu?"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Character** | Alluri Sitarama Raju, Komaram Bheem & Jake |
| **Actor** | Ram Charan, N. T. Rama Rao Jr. & Edward Sonnenblick |
| **Director** | S. S. Rajamouli |

**Context:** At a British colonial garden party in 1920s Delhi, an arrogant officer mocks Bheem for tripping on the dance floor—prompting Raju and Bheem to unleash a high-voltage mass dance duel.

**Why it endures:** This line kicks off "Naatu Naatu," which took American theaters and Netflix by storm and made history by winning the Academy Award for Best Original Song.
