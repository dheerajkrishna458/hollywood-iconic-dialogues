# The Terminator (1984)

> **The Terminator:** "I'm a friend of Sarah Connor. I was told she's here. Could I see her, please?"
>
> **Desk Sergeant:** "No, you can't see her. She's making a statement."
>
> **The Terminator:** "Where is she?"
>
> **Desk Sergeant:** "Look, it may take a while. Want to wait? There's a bench over there."
>
> **The Terminator:** "I'll be back."

| | |
|---|---|
| **Character** | The Terminator (T-800) |
| **Actor** | Arnold Schwarzenegger |
| **Director** | James Cameron |

**Context:** The machine tells the police station desk officer it will return, then drives a car through the lobby.

**Why it endures:** Three flat words that became Schwarzenegger's global calling card—making him a household action icon from American multiplexes to every corner of India.
