# Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone (2001)

> **Rubeus Hagrid:** "Harry—yer a wizard."
>
> **Harry Potter:** "I'm a what?"
>
> **Rubeus Hagrid:** "A wizard. And a thumpin' good 'un, I'd wager, once you've been trained up a bit."
>
> **Harry Potter:** "No, you've made a mistake. I mean… I can't be a wizard. I mean, I'm just Harry. Just Harry."

| | |
|---|---|
| **Character** | Rubeus Hagrid & Harry Potter |
| **Actor** | Robbie Coltrane & Daniel Radcliffe |
| **Director** | Chris Columbus |

**Context:** On Harry's eleventh birthday in a storm-battered shack on the sea, Hagrid knocks down the door and finally delivers Harry's Hogwarts letter in person.

**Why it endures:** For millennials and Gen Z in both the US and India, these four words launched the defining fantasy franchise of childhood.
