# 3 Idiots (2009)

> **Rancho:** "Look, this heart of ours gets scared very easily. You have to trick it. However big the problem is, tell your heart: 'All Izz Well, buddy, All Izz Well!'"
>
> **Farhan:** "Does that solve the problem?"
>
> **Rancho:** "No, the problem isn't solved, but you gain the courage to face it! And remember—don't run behind success. Pursue excellence, and success will follow you, pants down!"

**Context:** At an elite engineering college, free-thinking student Rancho shares his personal mantra for handling academic stress and his philosophy on learning vs. grades.

**Why it endures:** *3 Idiots* is a landmark Indian film that became a global sleeper hit on streaming and college campuses worldwide, making "All Izz Well" and "Pursue excellence" beloved mottoes across engineering and corporate teams everywhere.
