# Apocalypse Now (1979)

> "I love the smell of napalm in the morning."

| | |
|---|---|
| **Character** | Lt. Col. Bill Kilgore |
| **Actor** | Robert Duvall |
| **Director** | Francis Ford Coppola |

**Context:** Kilgore surveys the beach after an air strike, calmly savouring the aftermath.

**Why it endures:** Darkly comic and chilling at once, it distils the film's view of war as madness.
