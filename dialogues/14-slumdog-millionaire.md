# Slumdog Millionaire (2008)

> **Prem Kumar:** "Twenty million rupees. For the ultimate prize, what is the third musketeer's name in Alexandre Dumas's book? Is it A: Aramis, B: Cardinal Richelieu, C: D'Artagnan, or D: Planchet? Never in my life have I seen anyone wait until the final question to use their 50:50. Now left with A: Aramis, or C: D'Artagnan."
>
> **Jamal Malik:** "I actually don't know, Prem. So I'm just going to guess. Smile for the camera, Prem. My final answer is… A. Aramis."
>
> **Caption on Screen:** "It is written."

**Context:** On the Indian edition of *Who Wants to Be a Millionaire?* (*Kaun Banega Crorepati*), 18-year-old Mumbai "chaiwala" Jamal Malik faces the final 20-million-rupee question while the whole country watches.

**Why it endures:** Blending the globally familiar *Who Wants to Be a Millionaire?* game-show tension ("Final answer?") with Mumbai street life and A. R. Rahman's Oscar-winning score ("Jai Ho"), it bridged Western and Indian pop culture.
