# 15 Iconic Movie Dialogues (US & India Team Edition)

A curated collection of 15 famous movie dialogues in [`dialogues/`](dialogues/), selected specifically for a cross-cultural team of **US and Indian teammates**. Rather than older regional classics that only one side might know, this list brings together **global blockbusters** that both Indian and American audiences grew up watching alongside **cross-cultural / Indian-global crossover hits** (*RRR*, *3 Idiots*, *Slumdog Millionaire*, *Life of Pi*).

Each file holds the iconic dialogue exchange, who said it, and a short note on the scene and why it resonates across both cultures.
